Hawaii Pineapple

I chose this recipe because it looked like a simple recipe. My family loves Pizza, thats why I chose to do this recipe.-My Mom "We should go to Italy to eat some Pizza, since Pizza is from Italy".

I’d recommend using freshly diced pineapples. It tastes fresher and much better than anything canned or frozen! Also, be sure to thoroughly pat dry the pineapple before adding it on the pizza, otherwise the juices will pool around the cheese as it bakes, making it soggy.

ingredients Amount Details Steps
Pizza Dough 1LB of store-bought or homemade dough (flour, yeast, warm water, olive oil, sugar, and salt 450°F and 475°F (or up to 500°F if using a pizza stone or baking steel). Let your pizza dough rest at room temperature if it is cold, then stretch or roll it out into a 12-inch round shape. Place the dough on a lightly oiled baking sheet, pizza pan, or a cornmeal-dusted pizza peel.).
Pizza Sauce ½ cup of tomato-based or marinara pizza sauce Prick the center lightly with a fork to keep large air bubbles from forming. Spread an even layer of pizza sauce over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust. Cover the sauce with an even layer of shredded mozzarella cheese.
Cheese 1.5 to 2 cups of shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese Distribute the diced ham or Canadian bacon evenly across the cheese. Scatter the well-drained, patted-dry pineapple chunks in between and over the ham (avoid piling on too much pineapple so the center does not get soggy). Add optional extras like crumbled bacon or a pinch of dried oregano.
Meat 1/2 cup of diced ham or Canadian bacon Bake in the preheated oven for 10 to 20 minutes (depending on your oven temperature and crust thickness) until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and lightly browned. • Brush the outer crust with melted garlic butter if desired. Let the pizza rest for 5 to 7 minutes before slicing so the cheese and toppings set.
Fruit 1/2 cup of pineapple chunks, tidbits, or rings (fresh or drained canned)
