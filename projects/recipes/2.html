Ceviche

I chose this recipe because it's a traditional dish from where my both parents were born. My family loves Ceviche. -My Dad "When I tried Ceviche in the USA, it was good, but when i'm in Peru, Ceviche is ten thousands times better then it is in the USA. ".

I'd recommend using freshly squeezed lime juice and freshly diced fish for ceviche. It tastes fresher and much better than using fish that has been sitting around for too long! Also, be sure to drain any excess liquid from the fish and other ingredients before serving, otherwise the extra juices can dilute the citrus marinade and affect the overall flavor and texture of the ceviche.

Finely mince jalapeño or serrano peppers (remove seeds for less heat).
ingredients Amount Details Steps
Seafood Cut the fish or shrimp into uniform, bite-sized pieces (about 3/4-inch or 1/2-inch squares) so the citrus cures the flesh evenly. (Note: Some styles prefer to briefly poach shrimp in boiling water for 1 to 2 minutes first for food safety). Select very fresh, firm white fish (such as sea bass, snapper, halibut, or cod) or peeled and deveined raw shrimp.
Citrus Juice Juice enough fresh limes (and sometimes a splash of lemon or orange juice) to completely submerge the seafood. Avoid bottled juice or pre-packaged options for the best flavor. Red onion (finely diced or feathered) is most common, though white or yellow onions can also be used.
Onion Finely chop. (Tip: You can toss the onions in a little lime and salt beforehand to remove harsh bitterness). Tomatoes: Dice ripe Roma or vine tomatoes (removing watery inner seeds if preferred).
Chiles Fresh spicy peppers like jalapeño, serrano, or ají limo.
