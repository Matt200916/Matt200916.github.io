Grace Hopper was an American computer scientist and a United States Navy officer. She worked on early computers, developed a compiler, and helped shape the COBOL programming language.

During World War II, she worked with the Harvard Mark I computer. She later worked on UNIVAC and advocated for programming languages that were easier for people to read.
